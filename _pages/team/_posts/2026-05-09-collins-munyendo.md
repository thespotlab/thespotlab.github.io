Collins Munyendo is currently a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Center for Effective Global Action at the University of California, Berkeley. Starting from Fall 2027, he will join the Department of Computer Science at the National University of Singapore where he will direct the SPOT Lab. He earned his PhD in Computer Science from the George Washington University. Collins has broad interests in security, privacy, and safety, particularly from a human-centered perspective. When not doing research, Collins likes to spend his free time hiking, watching an ungodly amount of sports, and recovering from his coffee addiction.
