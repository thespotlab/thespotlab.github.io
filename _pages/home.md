The **SPOT** (**S**ecurity, **P**rivacy, and **O**nline **T**rust) Lab is an interdisciplinary research group directed by <a href="https://collinsmunyendo.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Collins Munyendo</a> launching soon at the <a href="https://www.nus.edu.sg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">National University of Singapore</a>. The lab works on security, privacy, and safety from a human-centered perspective. We use computational and social science methods to **spot** security, privacy, and safety challenges in existing and emerging technologies, then design and rigorously evaluate effective solutions. Some current directions we are exploring include:
- **User Authentication**: Passwords (including password-less options like passkeys) remain unusable in practice. We are interested in making authentication systems both usable and secure for users.
- **Underserved Users**: Most security and privacy research has focused on specific populations, limiting generalizability of solutions. We are interested in addressing the needs of various underserved populations around the world, for instance majority world users.
- **Organizational Security**: Organizations, and especially small and medium-sized organizations (SMEs), are often resource-contrained and may not prioritize security. We are interested in understanding the needs of SMEs and designing solutions that address those needs.
