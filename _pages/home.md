The **SPOT** (**S**ecurity, **P**rivacy, and **O**nline **T**rust) Lab is an interdisciplinary research group directed by <a href="https://collinsmunyendo.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Collins Munyendo</a> launching in Fall 2027 in the <a href="https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/cs/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Department of Computer Science</a> at the <a href="https://www.nus.edu.sg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">National University of Singapore</a>. The lab broadly works on digital security, privacy, and safety from a human-centered perspective. We use a diverse set of computational and social science methods to **spot** security, privacy, safety, and usability challenges in both existing and emerging technologies, then design and rigorously evaluate solutions that address those challenges. Some current directions we are exploring include:
- **User Authentication**: Passwords (including password-less options like passkeys) remain unusable. We are interested in making these systems both usable and secure.
- **Underserved Users**: Most security and privacy research has focused on specific populations, limiting generalizability of solutions. We are interested in addressing the needs of various underserved groups around the world such as majority world users.
- **Organizational Security**: Organizations, especially small and medium-sized organizations (SMEs), are resource-contrained and may not prioritize security. We are interested in understanding the needs of SMEs and designing solutions that address those needs.
